## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-octane empowerment, modern rebellion, and technical camaraderie. Crafted specifically for an adventurous women’s motorcycling collective, the visual direction merges high-performance street aesthetics with radical inclusivity and fierce confidence.

The design movement combines **High-Contrast Minimalism** with **Cyber-Industrial Performance**. It draws inspiration from night rides, illuminated dash clusters, and technical gear:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep asphalt tones, electric luminescence, and crisp, uncompromising clarity.
- **Tone & Mood:** Unapologetic, kinetic, precision-engineered, and protective.
- **Visual Tenets:** Pure contrast over clutter. Electric neon accents guide the eye to interactive focal points, while structural framing evokes motorcycle chassis and telemetry screens.

## Colors

The color palette is built around night visibility, high-contrast legibility, and technical motorsport aesthetics.

- **Primary (`#00FF66` — High-Vis Volt):** The core interactive driver. Used for primary CTAs, active route states, critical badges, and selective illuminated accents. Commands immediate visual priority.
- **Secondary (`#2A2A2A` — Forged Slate):** Structural surface color for mid-tier containers, input fills, and inactive interactive elements.
- **Tertiary (`#00E5FF` — Cyan Pulse):** An auxiliary telemetry accent reserved for navigational tracking, ride stats, and status indicators.
- **Neutral Base (`#121212` — Asphalt Black):** Deep, non-reflective ground surface. Minimizes eye fatigue in low-light environments.
- **Tonal Neutrals:**
  - Dark Surface / Elevated Base: `#181818`
  - Subtle Border / Divider: `#262626`
  - High-Contrast Text: `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary Text / Metadata: `#A3A3A3`
  - Muted Text / Inactive: `#525252`

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive, mechanical rhythm. 

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides technological sharpness, structural geometry, and an avant-garde motorsport edge. Upper display levels adopt tight letter spacing to convey speed and density.
- **Body & Longform (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers clean, contemporary geometry with high x-height and open counters, ensuring optimal legibility against dark slate surfaces under varying ambient lighting conditions.
- **Labels & Overlines (Space Grotesk):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.05em` to `0.1em`) to mimic dash instrumentation, telemetry tags, and flight-check markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows an asymmetrical fluid grid system designed to feel dynamic yet strictly organized:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Modules break standard containment with deliberate horizontal spans (e.g., 7/5 splits for imagery vs. telemetry data).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Side-by-side modules collapse into stacked pairing blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Full-bleed full-width cards with internal edge-to-edge swipes for route galleries and rider profiles.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Adheres to a strict 4px/8px incremental logic. Content-dense sections (route data, telemetry) utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while editorial narratives and hero viewports leverage `space-xl` and `space-2xl` for uninhibited breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on generic drop shadows, this design system creates depth through **tonal stratification, low-contrast ghost borders, and selective neon back-glows**.

1. **Surface Hierarchy:**
   - **Ground Layer (`#121212`):** Primary canvas, background viewports.
   - **Mid Layer (`#181818` / `#1E1E1E`):** Static cards, passive feed containers, and section blocks. Framed with a 1px solid hairline border in `#2A2A2A`.
   - **Raised Layer (`#222222`):** Interactive menus, modals, and active card hover states.
2. **Luminescence & Glow:**
   - Interactive focus and hover states deploy a razor-thin neon border (`1px solid #00FF66`) matched with a tight ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(0, 255, 102, 0.35)`.
   - Critical warnings, active GPS route indicators, and live event badges use a pulsating `0 0 24px rgba(0, 255, 102, 0.45)`.
3. **Ghost Borders:**
   - Default cards never use drop shadows; structural separation is defined purely by crisp `#2A2A2A` boundaries.

## Shapes

The design system employs a tactical juxtaposition: **ultra-sharp structural geometry contrasted with ergonomic pill-shaped touch targets**.

- **Cards & Architectural Shells:** Utilize sharp, architectural forms (`0` to `4px` maximum corner radius) to evoke precision machinery, frame welds, and fairing cuts.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Chips, Badges):** Fully pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`, `9999px` / `rounded-full`). This mimics aerodynamic cockpit switches, grips, and technical gear elements, providing an inviting, tactile contrast against the crisp backdrop.
- **Media Containers:** Clean 90-degree corners with optional technical corner notches (chamfered cuts) for highlighted telemetry panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill container (`border-radius: 9999px`). Background in `#00FF66`, typography in `#121212` (`Space Grotesk`, bold, uppercase, tracked). Hover state shifts brightness and emits an electric aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 255, 102, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary:** Pill container with 1px border in `#333333`, surface `#1E1E1E`, typography `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions border to `#00FF66` and text to `#00FF66`.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent fill, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle underline on hover with a leading `#00FF66` arrow icon.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (Live Ride, Lead Road Captain):** Pill-shaped, semi-translucent slate fill (`rgba(42, 42, 42, 0.8)`), 1px stroke in `#00FF66`. Includes a 6px circular pulsing indicator dot.
- **Filter Chips:** High-density pill toggles. Inactive state: `#1E1E1E` background, `#A3A3A3` text. Active state: solid `#00FF66` background, `#121212` text.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Pitch-black or dark slate base (`#181818`), sharp corners (`0px` or `2px`), bounded by a subtle `1px solid #262626` outline.
- **Interactive Ride Cards:** On hover, top border turns into a 2px `#00FF66` illuminated accent line; card elevates slightly with an internal border shift to `#333333`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Dark field fill (`#1E1E1E`), bottom hairline border (`2px solid #333333`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Focus transitions the bottom border instantly to `#00FF66` with a zero-delay snap. Labels sit uppercase above fields in `label-sm` Space Grotesk.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators for checkboxes, circular for radios. Inactive border `#333333`. Checked state fills `#00FF66` with `#121212` icons.

### Specialized Community Components
- **Ride Telemetry HUD:** Modular horizontal bar showing route difficulty, surface type, total distance, and pace. Rendered in monospaced/Space Grotesk metrics with neon accents.
- **Rider Profile Crest:** Sharp hexagonal or circular avatar framed by a dynamic neon ring indicating community tier (e.g., Solo Adventurer, Iron Butt, Road Captain).
